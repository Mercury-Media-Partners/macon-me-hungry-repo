# MACON ME HUNGRY DIRECTORY SERVICES AGREEMENT (HEADLINER TIER)

This Directory Services Agreement (the **"Agreement"**) is entered into as of the date signed below (the **"Effective Date"**), by and between **Macon Me Hungry LLC** ("Macon Me Hungry", "we", "us", or "our") and the undersigned business entity (**"Advertiser"** or "you").

---

### 1. PLATFORM & SERVICES
* **Macon Me Hungry** owns and operates the digital directory located at `maconmehungry.com` (the **"Platform"**).
* **Advertiser** desires to secure a premium listing on the Platform under the **Headliner Tier** to promote its business services, events, and location details to Platform visitors.

---

### 2. HEADLINER TIER SERVICES & SCOPE
During the Term of this Agreement, Macon Me Hungry shall provide the following features on the Platform for the Advertiser’s listing page:
1. **Total Search Dominance:** Permanent pinning at the top of all category search results. Featured rotation placement in the homepage "Spotlight" banner.
2. **Action-Oriented Conversion CTA:** A custom conversion button placed prominently on the listing detail page (e.g., "Buy Tickets", "Book a Table", "Reserve Consultation") linking directly to Advertiser's POS/Ticketing system.
3. **Unlimited Events:** Unlimited event publishing rights on the platform calendar, supporting both recurring events and high-ticket one-off tournament specials.
4. **Unlimited Jobs:** Unrestricted access to post job openings on the jobs board.
5. **Video Integration:** Capability to embed an autoplaying promotional video header (via YouTube/Vimeo) in place of a static hero image.

---

### 3. TERM & CANCELLATION POLICY
* **Initial Term:** This Agreement is valid for **one (1) year** from the Effective Date.
* **Non-Cancelable Initial Term:** Due to the extensive promotional and engineering work required upfront to onboard, optimize, and configure a Headliner listing and its custom assets, **this contract is non-cancelable** during the initial one-year Term.
* **Auto-Renewal:** At the end of the initial Term, this Agreement will automatically renew on a month-to-month basis at the then-current monthly rate, unless Advertiser provides written notice of cancellation at least thirty (30) days prior to the renewal date.

---

### 4. CONTENT & INTELLECTUAL PROPERTY LICENSE
* **Grant of License:** Advertiser grants Macon Me Hungry a non-exclusive, worldwide, royalty-free license to use, display, reproduce, and translate (English/Spanish) the business name, logos, taglines, hours, menus, images, video assets, and event details provided by Advertiser for placement on the Platform.
* **Accuracy:** Advertiser represents that all listing details provided are accurate, represent active operating hours, and do not infringe on any third-party intellectual property rights.

---

### 5. LIMITATION OF LIABILITY
* **Service Availability:** Macon Me Hungry strives for maximum uptime but does not guarantee uninterrupted or error-free operation of the Platform.
* **Maximum Liability:** To the maximum extent permitted by law, Macon Me Hungry’s total liability for any claim arising out of this Agreement is strictly limited to the total fees paid by the Advertiser to Macon Me Hungry during the three (3) months preceding the claim.
