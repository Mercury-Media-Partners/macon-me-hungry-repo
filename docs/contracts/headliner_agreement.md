# OUTATL DIRECTORY SERVICES AGREEMENT (HEADLINER TIER)

This Directory Services Agreement (the **"Agreement"**) is entered into as of the date signed below (the **"Effective Date"**), by and between **OutATL LLC** ("OutATL", "we", "us", or "our") and the undersigned business entity (**"Advertiser"** or "you").

---

### 1. RECITALS
* **OutATL** owns and operates the bilingual LGBTQ+ digital directory and World Cup visitor portal located at `outatl.com` (the **"Platform"**).
* **Advertiser** desires to secure a premium listing on the Platform under the **Headliner Tier** to secure dominant search rankings and conversion CTAs on the Platform.

---

### 2. HEADLINER TIER SERVICES & SCOPE
During the Term of this Agreement, OutATL shall provide the following features on the Platform for the Advertiser’s listing page:
1. **Total Search Dominance:** Permanent pinning at the top of all category search results. Featured rotation placement in the homepage "Spotlight" banner.
2. **Action-Oriented Conversion CTA:** A custom conversion button placed prominently on the listing detail page (e.g., "Buy Tickets", "Book a Table", "Reserve Consultation") linking directly to Advertiser's POS/Ticketing system.
3. **Unlimited Events:** Unlimited event publishing rights on the platform calendar, supporting both recurring events and high-ticket one-off tournament specials.
4. **Rich Media Integration:** Support for high-fidelity background videos, header video embeds (YouTube/Vimeo), and unlimited media gallery photos.
5. **Competitor Ad-Blocking:** Complete removal of all network advertisements, competitor suggestions, and "Similar Businesses" blocks from the Advertiser's listing page.

---

### 3. FEES, BILLING & TERM
* **Subscription Fee:** **$99.00 USD per Month (Founder Grandfather Rate)** (or **$699.00 USD per Year** if paid annually).
* **Billing Cycle:** Billed automatically on the Effective Date and recurring monthly/annually thereafter to the payment method on file.
* **Term:** This Agreement is on a month-to-month (or year-to-year) basis, commencing on the Effective Date.
* **Termination:** Either party may cancel the subscription at any time. Cancellation will take effect at the end of the current billing cycle. No refunds will be provided for partial billing periods.
