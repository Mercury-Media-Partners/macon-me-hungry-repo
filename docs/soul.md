# The Soul of Macon Me Hungry: Local Flavor, Visibility, and Stewardship

Macon Me Hungry is not just a commercial business directory; it is a digital anchor for Macon's vibrant culinary economy, culture, and community. 

---

## 🌟 The Core Mission

To capture the growing enthusiasm for local dining and route that attention directly to the locally-owned and independent spaces that form the backbone of Macon's community. We exist to make sure the local food economy thrives and that locals and visitors alike can easily discover the best bites in town.

---

## 🛡️ Pillars of the Project

### 1. Radically Inclusive Intent Segmentation
By implementing the **"Dual State" Master Switch** (`☀️ Out & About` vs. `🌙 After Hours`), we treat day-to-day community spaces with the same prestige as nightlife. Bakeries, cafes, and food trucks are elevated, protecting them from being buried by mega-restaurants.

### 2. High-Contrast "Studio Noir" Aesthetics
Our design system is dark, premium, and electric. We use deep background hues, vibrant neon glows, and micro-interactions. The visual identity reflects the energy of Macon's culinary revival and the high-end curation of its daytime lifestyle.

### 3. Economic Stewardship & Transparence
Every business detail page features an **Impact Dashboard** visualizing jobs supported, Georgia-sourced produce/materials, and year of establishment. We celebrate local longevity and community integration, proving that spending money at these venues is an investment in Macon's future.

### 4. Zero-Friction Discovery
Macon is a growing city welcoming people from all over. We build seamless discovery directly into the routing layer, ensuring that everyone can access resources and directory maps intuitively to find exactly what they're hungry for.
