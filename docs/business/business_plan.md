# OutATL: Business Plan & Monetization Strategy

This document serves as the official reference for the monetization, expansion, and design strategy of **OutATL** (formerly AltATL), Atlanta's premier bilingual directory and community hub for the LGBTQ+ economy and international visitors (optimized for the 2026 World Cup).

For actual shipped tier pricing, see `docs/contracts/` (source of truth) and `docs/business/value_ladder_evolution.md` (iteration history).

## 🚀 The Core Vision: From Nightlife to Queer Hub

By pivoting from a narrow "Nightlife Directory" to a comprehensive **"Atlanta Queer Hub" (OutATL)**, the platform expands its total addressable market (TAM) by **300%**. The queer economy is built on community hubs: places to study, shop, train, and network.

### Target Non-Nightlife Sectors
*   **Lifestyle & Cafes**: Coffee shops, bakeries, bookstores, co-working spots.
*   **Professional Services**: Real estate agents, mortgage brokers, tax professionals, attorneys, therapists, doctors.
*   **Wellness & Fitness**: Gyms, personal trainers, barbershops, salons, spas.
*   **Retail**: Queer-owned boutiques, galleries, thrift stores, local brands.

## 🗣️ The Pitch Playbook (Alisha Conlin Hurd Method)

When selling listings, bar owners and professionals will reject generic "advertising." Pitch **measurable results and community integration**.

### The Mid-Tier Pitch (Bars & Cafes)
> *"Don't buy an ad; buy butts in seats. When tourists visit for Atlanta Pride, Black Pride, or the World Cup, they don't know the local neighborhood Instagram handles. They Google 'Best queer bars in Atlanta' or 'Queer friendly cafes East Atlanta.' We capture that high-intent search traffic and direct it to your Tuesday night trivia or weekend brunch. Just 2-3 extra customers in a month covers the entire $49 subscription."*

### The Premium-Tier Pitch (Clubs & Circuit Parties)
> *"We place a 'Buy Tickets' button right on your listing, stripping out all competitor listings nearby. If our directory drives just four ticket sales for a $40 cover Saturday night circuit party, the $149 monthly subscription has fully paid for itself. The rest is pure profit."*

### The Professional Services Pitch (Real Estate, Doctors, Gyms)
> *"We build community integration. When people move to Atlanta for work, they search for trusted professional services on Day One. Under our Out & About state, you won't be buried by late-night club listings. You own the premium slot at the top of your category when search intent is highest."*

## 🎨 UI Design Concept: The "Dual State" Master Switch

To segment visitor intent and protect paid monetization, we implement a prominent, high-fidelity **Master Switch** at the top of the interface.

```
                  +-----------------------------------+
                  |  ☀️ OUT & ABOUT  |  🌙 AFTER HOURS  |
                  +-----------------------------------+
```

### State A: ☀️ Out & About (Day Mode)
*   **Target Audience**: Residents and visitors looking for daily life essentials.
*   **Surfaced Sectors**: Lifestyle, cafes, retail, wellness, professional services.
*   **Category Pills**: Cafes, Barbers, Gyms, Real Estate, Doctors, Bookstores.
*   **Monetization Protection**: Removes nightlife clutter. An LGBTQ+ real estate agent pays for a premium listing knowing they aren't competing with 2 AM clubs.

### State B: 🌙 After Hours (Night Mode)
*   **Target Audience**: Partygoers, tourists, and weekend night-outers.
*   **Surfaced Sectors**: Nightlife, entertainment, events, dining.
*   **Category Pills**: Bars, Clubs, Late Night Eats, Drag Shows, Pop-ups.
*   **Monetization Protection**: Nightclubs own the spotlight during weekend evening traffic without diluting their brand with office hours or retail.
