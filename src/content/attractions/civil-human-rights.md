The National Center for Civil and Human Rights is an iconic cultural institution located in downtown Atlanta, adjacent to Centennial Olympic Park. The museum features powerful immersive exhibitions that connect the history of the American Civil Rights Movement with modern global struggles for human rights.

The Center regularly highlights LGBTQ+ human rights advocacy and hosts community panels, educational programs, and pride events that celebrate progress and explore ongoing civil rights advocacy globally.
