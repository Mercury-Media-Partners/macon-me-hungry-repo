Woodruff Park is a vibrant 6-acre park situated in the heart of Downtown Macon, serving as a popular lunchtime spot and gathering area for students, locals, and visitors. Originally established in 1973, it is named after legendary Coca-Cola leader Robert W. Woodruff.

The park offers a rich urban landscape, including the iconic 'ATL' playground structure, a massive public water fountain, and open lawn areas. Located directly adjacent to GSU (Georgia State University) and the Five Points MARTA Station, it serves as an excellent central orientation point for travelers exploring downtown or heading west to the Mercedes-Benz Stadium.
