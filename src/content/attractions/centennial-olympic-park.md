Centennial Olympic Park was created as a central gathering place for visitors and locals during the 1996 Summer Olympic Games. Today, this 22-acre downtown park serves as a hub of civic life and will host the official FIFA Fan Festival for the World Cup 2026. 

Walk along the Olympic ring brick pathways, enjoy the Fountain of Rings show, or relax on the expansive lawns before heading to Mercedes-Benz Stadium, which is only a short 10-minute walk away. It is the perfect central landmark for transit access and pre-game assembly.
