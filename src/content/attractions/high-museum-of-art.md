The High Museum of Art, located in the heart of Midtown's arts district, is the leading art museum in the Southeastern United States. With a collection of more than 18,000 works of art, the High is dedicated to supporting and exhibiting a diverse range of artistic voices.

The High hosts popular LGBTQ+ friendly events, including their high-energy "High Frequency Fridays" featuring live DJs, drinks, and late-night gallery tours, making it a beloved cultural hub for the local queer community and art lovers alike.
