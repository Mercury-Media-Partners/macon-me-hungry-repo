**Curator's Note:** The atmosphere at Centennial Olympic Park is absolutely electric! As defending World Cup champs Argentina took on Switzerland, the FIFA Fan Festival was packed with thousands of fans braving the afternoon rain. A huge congratulations to England for punching their ticket to the semi-finals right here in Macon after a nail-biting extra-time victory over Norway!

### Full Broadcast Transcript

The winner is going to be headed right here to Macon to play **England** in the semifinals. One step closer to World Cup glory. Reporter **Justin Barker** is live from Centennial Olympic Park."

**[Justin Barker Reporting]**

It was definitely an England-heavy crowd earlier. Of course, there were Norwegian fans here, but the majority of the thousands of people in Centennial Olympic Park were just there to watch a good soccer match.

We had a solid amount of rain up until that point, which delayed the opening half. But it was a big payoff for those England fans living here in Macon to stick around in that weather."
