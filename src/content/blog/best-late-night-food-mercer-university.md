When the library closes and the late-night cravings hit, Mercer University students know exactly where to go. Macon might not be a 24/7 city, but the late-night food scene near campus is legendary. 

### 1. The Rookery
Open late on weekends, The Rookery is an absolute institution. You haven't really lived in Macon until you've had a Jimmy Carter Burger at midnight. The vibe is laid back, the music is always good, and the fries are crispy.

### 2. Ocmulgee Brewpub
While known for their craft beer, their kitchen stays open surprisingly late for downtown. Their gourmet burgers and hand-cut fries are the perfect fuel for a late-night study session or post-game celebration.

### Pro-Tip for Mercer Students
Always check the hours before you head out, especially on weeknights, as downtown Macon tends to wind down earlier Monday through Wednesday. But Thursday through Saturday? The night is yours!
