When the Georgia weather is cooperating, there is nothing better than a slow Sunday brunch on a beautiful patio. Macon has been stepping up its al fresco dining game, and we've rounded up the best spots to get your mimosa fix outdoors.

### 1. Dovetail
While Dovetail is famous for its upscale Southern dinners, their weekend brunch is a hidden gem. If you can score a spot near the windows or outdoor setup, the farm-to-table brunch menu (think duck confit hash and seasonal preserves) tastes even better in the sunshine.

### 2. Downtown Macon's Sidewalk Cafes
Many spots along Cherry Street and Mulberry Street throw open their doors and set up sidewalk seating on Saturday mornings. Grabbing a coffee and a pastry while people-watching downtown is a classic Macon weekend tradition.

Remember to call ahead on beautiful spring weekends, as patio tables fill up quickly!
