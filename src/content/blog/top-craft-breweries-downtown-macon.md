Macon's craft beer scene has exploded over the last few years, turning downtown into a legitimate destination for beer tourism. If you're looking for a great pint, here are the top breweries you need to visit.

### 1. Ocmulgee Brewpub
Located in the heart of downtown, Ocmulgee Brewpub pairs incredible craft beer with some of the best burgers in the city. Their flagship IPA is a local favorite, and the spacious, industrial-chic interior makes it a perfect gathering spot.

### 2. Fall Line Brewing Co.
Named after the geographic fall line that runs right through Macon, this brewery offers a massive indoor space and an incredibly popular outdoor beer garden. It's dog-friendly, family-friendly during the day, and their lagers are exceptionally crisp.

### 3. Piedmont Brewery & Kitchen
Piedmont isn't just a brewery; it's an experience. With a full arcade downstairs and a fantastic barbecue menu upstairs, their rotating taps ensure there is always something new and experimental to try. 

Take a walking tour of all three next time you're downtown—they are all within a few blocks of each other!
