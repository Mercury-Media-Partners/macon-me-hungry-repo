Piedmont Brewery & Kitchen is a massive two-story operation in the heart of downtown Macon. Upstairs, you'll find an incredible urban barbecue restaurant and a rotating list of house-brewed craft beers. Downstairs, you'll find a massive arcade with classic pinball and cabinet games. It's the perfect spot for families during the day and adults at night.
