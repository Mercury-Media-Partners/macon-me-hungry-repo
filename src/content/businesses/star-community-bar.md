The Star Community Bar, affectionately known as Star Bar, is a Little Five Points landmark and a staple of Atlanta's live music and comedy scenes since 1991. Famous for its retro dive vibes, legendary basement comedy nights, and rock-and-roll shows, it is a beloved neighborhood institution. Gritty, welcoming, and endlessly entertaining, it remains a must-visit spot for locals and music lovers.
