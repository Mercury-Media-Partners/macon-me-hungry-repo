We didn't name this bar Peachtree & Peachtree to confuse you. We named it to welcome you — because if you've lived in Atlanta for any amount of time, you already know the joke. Founded in 2019, the Social Club is Buckhead's answer to a neighborhood bar that takes its cocktails seriously but never takes itself too seriously. We sponsor two LGBTQ+ nonprofit events every quarter, source our spirits locally when possible, and have never, not once, correctly given someone directions using Peachtree Road.
