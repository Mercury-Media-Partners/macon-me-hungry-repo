Nu-Way Weiners is an absolute Macon institution. Established in 1916, it is the second oldest hot dog stand in the United States. Famous for its iconic bright red hot dogs, chili, and flaky biscuits, it's a must-visit for anyone looking for authentic, historic Macon flavor.
