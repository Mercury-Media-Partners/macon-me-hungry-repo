529 EAV is a legendary live music venue and dive bar located in the heart of East Atlanta Village. Known for its gritty, high-energy atmosphere, friendly bartenders, and stellar sound system, it plays host to some of the best local and national indie bands, punk acts, and electronic music. With a cozy back patio and strong, affordable drinks, it is a vital cornerstone of the EAV nightlife scene.
