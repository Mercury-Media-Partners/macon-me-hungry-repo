Intown Primary Care is Atlanta's leading LGBTQ+-affirming medical center. Founded in 2002, the clinic specializes in comprehensive primary care, preventative wellness, HIV preventative care (PrEP), and compassionate hormone replacement therapies. Located in the heart of Midtown near Piedmont Park, Intown Primary Care is built on the principle of providing high-quality, non-judgmental healthcare in a welcoming environment. With a dedicated team of licensed physicians and support staff, the clinic is a critical wellness pillar for Atlanta's queer community.
