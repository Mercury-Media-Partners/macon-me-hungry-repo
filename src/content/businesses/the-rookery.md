Since its opening on July 1, 1976, The Rookery has served as a central gathering place in the city, known for its deep ties to Macon's music history and community culture. The restaurant is widely recognized for its signature burgers (like the "Jimmy Carter Burger") and unique, over-the-top milkshakes.
