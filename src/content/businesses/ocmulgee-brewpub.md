Known for its "Macon made" beer and gourmet burgers, Ocmulgee Brewpub is a well-regarded local destination for food and drink. The pub specializes in handcrafted beers—ranging from lagers and IPAs to German classics and seasonal experimental brews. The space features a clean, industrial-inspired design that incorporates historic architectural elements of downtown Macon.
