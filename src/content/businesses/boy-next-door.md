Boy Next Door Menswear is a Midtown Atlanta landmark. Since opening its doors in 1980, it has styled generations of the city's LGBTQ+ community and visitors alike. Known for its curated collections of boutique activewear, underwear, high-contrast streetwear, and the city's premier selection of pool-party swimwear, it remains a central destination for people seeking bold, self-expressive fashion. Located just minutes from Piedmont Park in the historic Ansley area, BND is staffed by helpful stylists who have made it a trusted name for over forty years.
