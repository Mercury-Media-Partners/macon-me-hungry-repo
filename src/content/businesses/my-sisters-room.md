My Sister's Room (MSR) has been the heartbeat of the Southeast's queer women's community since 1996. Located in the lively Crescent Avenue district of Midtown, MSR offers bi-level dance floors, a spacious outdoor patio deck, drag king showcases, comedy nights, and a high-energy crowd dancing to the city's top DJs.
