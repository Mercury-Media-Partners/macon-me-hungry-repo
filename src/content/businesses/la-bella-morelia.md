Tucked away on Mulberry Street, La Bella Morelia is an absolute hidden gem. They serve undeniably the most authentic Mexican street food in Macon. The portions are generous, the salsas are fresh and fiery, and the quesabirria tacos have a cult following. Don't let the unassuming storefront fool you—this is top-tier food.
