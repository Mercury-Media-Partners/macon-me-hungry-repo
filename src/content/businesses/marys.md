Mary's is the beating heart of East Atlanta's queer scene. Unlike the polished clubs of Midtown, Mary's is gloriously rough around the edges — sticky floors, cheap drinks, and drag performances that will leave you speechless. If you're visiting for the World Cup and want the real Atlanta, skip the tourist trail and come here.
