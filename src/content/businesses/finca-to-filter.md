FiNCA to FiLTER was founded to celebrate queer culture through craft coffee. Located in the Old Fourth Ward neighborhood, this vibrant cafe focuses on local sourcing, radical hospitality, and creating a space where the community can gather, work, and connect. Known for its rotating selection of coffees from queer- and minority-owned roasters, a beautiful outdoor patio, and regular community events, it has quickly become a beloved neighborhood anchor.
