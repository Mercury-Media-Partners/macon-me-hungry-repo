7 Stages Theatre is a historic, non-profit performing arts center in Little Five Points, founded in 1979. Known for its focus on social, political, and spiritual issues, it hosts avant-garde plays, international theatre exchanges, and community-driven events. It is a vital hub for creative expression and dialogue in Atlanta.
