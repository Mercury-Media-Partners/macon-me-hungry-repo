Traffic on the BeltLine is Atlanta's most ironic coffee shop — named after the thing everyone hates, located where that thing literally cannot reach you. We opened in 2021 with a simple mission: give Atlantans a place to breathe, laugh about their commute, and get genuinely excellent coffee. Our beans rotate seasonally from queer and minority-owned roasters across Latin America. Our patio opens directly onto the BeltLine trail. We have never, not once, had a parking problem.
